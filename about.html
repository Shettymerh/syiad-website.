About SYIAD

Building Integrity, Promoting Peace and Empowering African Youths for Sustainable Development.

Identity & Purpose

Who We Are

The Society for Youths Integrity and African Development (SYIAD) is a Non-Governmental Organization (NGO) dedicated to propagating moral uprightness, building enduring peace, and fostering meaningful collaboration among youths across the African continent and globally.

Operating from Maiduguri, Borno State, Nigeria, SYIAD functions as an institutional platform bringing youths together to address critical societal challenges, promote conflict resolution, uphold cultural heritage, and facilitate educational advancement.

Our commitment is rooted in the conviction that sustainable African development is inextricably linked to the ethical integrity, peaceful disposition, and mutual understanding demonstrated by its youth population.

Constitutional Mandate

Our Objectives

The Society for Youths Integrity and African Development is committed to the following five fundamental objectives:

1
Objective 1: Propagating honesty and integrity among African youths.
2
Objective 2: Promoting peace, preventing conflict, and facilitating conflict resolution.
3
Objective 3: Partnering with government and non-governmental organizations to promote peace and foster understanding of the virtues of harmonious relationships, irrespective of ethnic, racial, or geographical differences.
4
Objective 4: Promoting education and upholding cultural heritage for sustainable development.
5
Objective 5: Promoting mutual understanding and cooperation between African youths and youths across the globe.

Institutional Transparency Notice

In accordance with our core commitment to integrity, SYIAD publishes its constitutional objectives and leadership registry transparently. Specific project histories, donor registries, and statistical evaluations will be officially published as program documentation is formally cleared by the Executive Council.
